Analytical Scope

Project Purpose & Objectives

Fuel prices are a pivotal determinant of inflation, logistical viability, and cost of living. This project investigates why pump prices deviate drastically across neighboring sovereign nations.

Cross-Country Disparities

Compare retail gasoline and diesel prices across geographic regions to highlight regional disparities driven by geographic proximity to refineries and domestic reserves.

Taxation vs Subsidies

Disentangle how domestic fiscal policies—from carbon excise taxes to sovereign consumer subsidies—create artificial price barriers or safety nets for end consumers.

Crude Price Pass-Through

Track temporal correlations between Brent Crude international price shifts and domestic consumer price volatility across varied national income tiers.
